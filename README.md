# StockSense — Inventory Management System (IMS)

StockSense is a modular, real-time inventory-management workspace designed to digitize and streamline all stock-related operations for businesses, replacing manual registers and scattered spreadsheets[cite: 1].

---

## 🚀 Product Purpose & Overview

StockSense provides a centralized platform for inventory managers and warehouse staff to oversee products, track warehouse balances, and process operational movements seamlessly[cite: 1]. 

* **Target Users**: Inventory Managers (managing incoming and outgoing stock) and Warehouse Staff (performing transfers, picking, shelving, and counting)[cite: 1].
* **Core Capabilities**:
  * Product and SKU cataloging with categories, units of measure, and reorder thresholds[cite: 1].
  * Multi-warehouse and location management[cite: 1].
  * End-to-end operational workflows: Receipts, Deliveries, Internal Transfers, and Physical-count Adjustments[cite: 1].
  * Real-time movement ledger and audit trail for every validated stock change[cite: 1].
  * Dashboard analytics, alerts for low/out-of-stock items, and profile controls[cite: 1].

---

## 📦 Core Features & Workflows

1. **Product Management**: Create and update master items with names, SKUs, categories, units of measure, and reorder rules[cite: 1].
2. **Receipts (Incoming Goods)**: Record shipments arriving from vendors, input quantities, and validate them to automatically increase stock balances[cite: 1].
3. **Delivery Orders (Outgoing Goods)**: Process customer shipments by tracking picked and packed items, validating orders to safely decrease source stock[cite: 1].
4. **Internal Transfers**: Relocate stock between warehouses or internal storage locations while logging every ledger movement[cite: 1].
5. **Stock Adjustments**: Fix discrepancies between recorded stock and physical counts by inputting counted quantities to trigger auto-updates and signed ledger adjustments[cite: 1].
